## Corrections in this working tree

* The V=9 experiments now distinguish failed certificate searches from proved feasibility or proved
  non-existence of certificates. Their extrapolation is identified as a sample-based estimate.
* The V=8 depth observation is stated about the stored certificate assignment, without claiming that
  one-cone certificates are impossible. The V=7 replication is identified as numerical evidence.
* The frustum thickness experiments and tendency towards flat shapes are described as numerical
  observations; neither establishes a global thickness optimum or a lower bound on the vertex count.
* Appendix B gives explicit orientation determinants. At genuine pyramid boundaries a tetrahedron
  can be flat; the formulas extend continuously with no sign reversal. REVIEW_GUIDE.md now says so.
* The example `(u,v,w)=(1,1,1)` was removed from the list of degenerate points in D_Z: it is outside
  the restricted domain `uvw <= 1/2`.
* The requirements comment uses A–B–C numbering; the audit directory descriptions mention both audits.
