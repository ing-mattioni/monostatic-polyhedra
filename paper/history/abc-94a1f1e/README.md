# Historical unpublished A/B/C manuscript

These files preserve the manuscript at repository commit 94a1f1e, before the
fourteen-vertex rewrite on 17 November 2026. They are not the current paper and
are not included in the new Zenodo package.

The former Theorem C is the construction now presented as Theorem 1 in the
current paper. The former A/B results were removed from the publication scope
after comparison with prior public research material. Their old priority,
literature and publication-status statements must not be treated as current.
In particular, wording about an archived release referred to a prepared draft;
the Zenodo record had not been published.

The old full verification workflow is preserved at repository root as
reproduce_abc.py, with CHECKSUMS.sha256 and requirements-verify.txt.
It is separate from the C-only release and does not establish novelty.
